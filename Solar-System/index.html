  • Body sizes: Enhanced mode enlarges bodies independently. Relative mode uses one common radius scale for the Sun, planets, and moons. Select a body to inspect it closely.
  • Distances: Planetary orbits share an AU scale, separate from body sizes. Moon orbits are spread out for visibility. Neither mode shows body sizes and orbital distances on one physical scale.
  • Inventory: Moons, belts, and rings are illustrative selections. Body cards identify reused textures.
  • Rate: At 1×, one Earth year passes in 60 real seconds.
  • Surface rotation: Planet spins are slowed 120× relative to orbital time for readability: Earth turns about once every 20 seconds at 1×. Surface orientations are illustrative.
